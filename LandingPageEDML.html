    Questions

    Before you start.

    Do I need to read music?

    No. By Ear is built around listening and playing. Nothing is written down, and you never have to name what you hear.

    What do I play on?

    The on-screen keyboard, with your mouse, your touchscreen or your computer keys. If you plug in a MIDI keyboard, By Ear listens to that instead.

    What level is it for?

    Every level. If you've never found a melody by ear, the first ones are three or four notes long. If you've been playing for years, the engine keeps raising the ceiling until the melodies are genuinely hard — nobody is stuck at a level.

    What kind of music is included?

    Classical and traditional repertoire: thousands of melodies, from Bach themes to folk tunes.

    How does the Adaptive Ear Engine™ work?

    It analyses your performance across several aspects of melodic hearing — pitch, intervals, memory, melodic jumps — and uses that profile to choose your next challenges.
